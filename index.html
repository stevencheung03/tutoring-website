Teaching Philosophy

1

Diagnose first. Understand exactly where understanding breaks down before teaching anything new.

2

Build from foundations. Advanced math fails when basics are shaky. We fix the root, not the symptom.

3

Teach thinking, not steps. Students who understand *why* a method works can adapt to any problem.

4

Celebrate progress. Confidence is built through visible improvement, not just perfect scores.

FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How do I know if my child needs a tutor?

Common signs include declining grades, avoidance of math homework, expressions of frustration or anxiety about math, spending excessive time on assignments without completion, or a teacher expressing concern. The free diagnostic assessment can also help clarify whether tutoring would be beneficial — there's no obligation to sign up.

What happens in the free consultation?

The 30-minute consultation includes a brief diagnostic assessment to identify current skill level and gaps, a discussion of goals and timeline, an overview of how sessions are structured, and a recommended plan. It's a no-pressure conversation — we want you to feel confident before committing.

Do sessions happen online or in person?

Both options are available. Online sessions use a shared digital whiteboard (similar to a shared iPad) along with video call, which many students actually prefer. In-person sessions are available in the [City] metro area. The quality is the same either way — choose what works best for your schedule and comfort.

How long until I see improvement?

Most students show noticeable improvement in confidence within 2–3 sessions. Grade improvements typically appear within 4–6 weeks of consistent tutoring, depending on the starting point and the gap being addressed. We track progress formally so you'll see the data, not just feel the difference.

What if my child doesn't click with the tutor?

The rapport between student and tutor is critical. If after the first two sessions you or your child feel it's not a good fit, we'll discuss what's not working and either adjust our approach or, if needed, refer you to a trusted colleague. Your success comes first — we never pressure families to continue if it's not working.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No contracts. The Starter plan is pay-per-session. The Growth and Intensive plans are billed monthly but can be cancelled at any time with no penalty. We believe the results speak for themselves — we don't need to lock you in.

Do you work with students with learning differences?

Yes. Dr. Chen has training and experience working with students with ADHD, dyscalculia, and processing differences. Sessions are adapted with multisensory techniques, shorter segments if needed, and explicit scaffolding. If your student has an IEP or 504 plan, we're happy to align our approach with it.

What's the cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations with less notice are charged at 50% of the session rate. We understand emergencies happen — we handle those on a case-by-case basis with understanding.
